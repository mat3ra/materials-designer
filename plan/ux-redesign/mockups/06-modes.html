One core, four costumes

Standalone IDE, platform-embedded editor, read-only viewer, and micro picker share one document model, one operation engine, and one component set. Only chrome and permissions change — nothing in the core may assume a costume. This is the contract that lets Materials Designer 2.0 also serve as the platform's MaterialGeometry view and its material picker, instead of three divergent implementations.
